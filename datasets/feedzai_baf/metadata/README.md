# Feedzai Bank Account Fraud (BAF) Dataset

The Bank Account Fraud (BAF) dataset suite consists of 6 synthetic datasets (Base and 5 Variants) generated from real-world anonymized bank account opening data. It was released by Feedzai at NeurIPS 2022 to address the lack of realistic, large-scale, and public datasets for fraud detection that incorporate bias and temporal shifts.

## Dataset Details
- **Total Records:** 6 million (1M per variant)
- **Features:** 30 realistic attributes (plus 'month' and 'target')
- **Fraud Rate:** ~1.10%
- **Temporal Scope:** 8 months of simulated data
